## 摘要
量化动物行为通常需要将连续动作分割为离散、可解释的状态，然而大多数自动化流程从关键点动态推断动作，并受限于关键点追踪质量。本文提出FERAL（动物运动识别的特征提取工具包），一种监督视频理解工具包，直接将原始视频映射到帧级行为标签，跳过了传统流程中的关键点提取和姿态分类阶段。在多项基准测试中，FERAL达到或超过了最先进的基于姿态和视频的基线方法。在CalMS21小鼠社交互动基准测试中，它仅使用四分之一的训练数据即超越了Google的VideoPrism。FERAL可跨物种（猿类、斑马、小鼠、蚂蚁、果蝇和线虫）、记录条件以及组织层级（从单个动物到社交互动乃至群体规模的集体行为）进行泛化。作为用户友好的开源软件包发布，它能与现有分析流程集成。通过直接从原始视频实现可扩展、物种无关的行为量化，FERAL拓宽了可用于研究动物行为的实验范式。

## Abstract
Quantifying animal behavior often requires segmenting continuous actions into discrete, interpretable states, yet most automated pipelines infer actions from keypoint dynamics and are limited by keypoint tracking quality. Here we present FERAL (Feature Extraction for Recognition of Animal Locomotion), a supervised video-understanding toolkit that maps raw video directly to frame-level behavioral labels, bypassing the keypoint-extraction and pose-classification stages of conventional pipelines. Across benchmarks, FERAL matches or exceeds state-of-the-art pose- and video-based baselines. On the CalMS21 mouse social-interaction benchmark, it exceeds Google's VideoPrism using only a quarter of the training data. FERAL generalizes across species (apes, zebras, mice, ants, flies, and nematodes), recording conditions, and levels of organization, from single animals to social interactions and colony-scale collective behavior. Released as a user-friendly, open-source package, it integrates with existing analysis pipelines. By enabling scalable, species-agnostic behavioral quantification directly from raw video, FERAL broadens the experimental paradigms available for studying animal behavior.
